Education
======
* Postdoc, IBIOMAR-CCT CONICET-CENPAT, Puerto Madryn, Argentina, 2024-present
  * Topic: Implementation of artificial intelligence tools for monitoring marine biodiversity in protected areas of Chubut.
  * Director: Dr. Gregorio Bigatti, Co-director: Dr. Enrique Montes Herrera
* Ph.D. in Biological Sciences, UNPSJB (Universidad Nacional de la Patagonia San Juan Bosco) - IBIOMAR, Puerto Madryn, Argentina, 2018-2024
  * Thesis: Biodiversity of epi-benthic communities along rocky reefs in the Patagonian Atlantic coast: current state and modeling under a climate change scenario.
  * Director: Dr. Gregorio Bigatti, Co-directors: Dr. Juan Pablo Livore, Dr. Philippe Archambault
* M.Sc. in Oceanography, UQAR (Université du Québec à Rimouski), Quebec, Canada, 2015-2018
  * Thesis: Short-term responses of Arctic deep-sea benthic macrofauna to pulses of phytodetritus.
  * Advisors: Christian Nozais and Philippe Archambault
* B.Sc. in Biological Sciences, UNPSJB (Universidad Nacional de la Patagonia San Juan Bosco), Puerto Madryn, Argentina, 2004-2013
  * Thesis: Recreational diving effects on benthic organisms in the underwater parks of Puerto Madryn, Chubut, Argentina.
  * Director: Dr. Gregorio Bigatti, Co-director: Federico Márquez
