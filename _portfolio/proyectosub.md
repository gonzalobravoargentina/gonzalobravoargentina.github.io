Co-founder and scientific diver at [Proyectosub](https://www.proyectosub.org.ar/), a citizen-science and outreach project working with marine invertebrates of the Atlantic coast of Patagonia (2013-2023). Activities included:

- Development of promotional and educational material, including [educational posters](https://www.proyectosub.org.ar/afiches/) on Patagonian marine invertebrates
- Coordination of "Proyectosub Sudamérica," an itinerant science outreach tour through Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Colombia, Venezuela, Brazil, Uruguay and Argentina
- Underwater photo/video database of Patagonian marine life
- Participation in the CONICET/TECtv documentary and [TV series](http://www.tectv.gob.ar/programacion-series/proyecto-sub)
- Oral presentations in schools, universities and scientific meetings
