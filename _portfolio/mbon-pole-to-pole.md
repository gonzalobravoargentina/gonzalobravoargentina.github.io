Research assistant and data manager for the [Marine Biodiversity Observation Network (MBON) Pole to Pole of the Americas](https://marinebon.github.io/p2p/members.html) since 2019, working on intertidal biodiversity surveys and the development of a sampling protocol for monitoring marine biodiversity on rocky shores using geo-referenced photoquadrats and Artificial Intelligence classification tools. Organized MBON Pole to Pole training workshops in Camarones (2023) and Ushuaia (2024), Argentina.

I also work on interactive dashboards for exploring and visualizing biodiversity monitoring data, such as the [Intertidal Monitoring Dashboard](https://mbon-poletopole.shinyapps.io/monitoreo-intermareal/).
