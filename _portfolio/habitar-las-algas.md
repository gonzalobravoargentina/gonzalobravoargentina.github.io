[Habitar las Algas](https://www.habitaralgas.com/) is an initiative exploring coexistence with Patagonian kelp forests through shared experiences, science, art, and ocean education.

Its coexistence residencies in Chubut and Ushuaia bring people together to explore coastal ecosystems and share knowledge. The project also shares these experiences through underwater photography, documentary videos, and immersive 360° educational activities.
